# How to delete your GainsLab account

## From the app (recommended)

1. Open the "You" sheet (profile icon).
2. Go to the **Account** section → **Delete account**.
3. If you are Pro, you will see a warning: your subscription is tied to the
   account and will be lost.
4. Optionally check **"Also delete the data on this device"** to remove your
   local training data too.
5. Enter your password and type **DELETE** to confirm.

What gets deleted: your `users/{your-id}` document (program, mesocycle,
session, exercises, templates, nutrition, cardio, weigh-ins, profile,
settings), your data documents (`data/*`, including session history), and your
auth user. The subscription document (`data/subscription`) is managed by an
external backend and is not touched from the app.

You need an internet connection and your current password. If the app asks you
to sign in again for security, do so and retry.

## By contact

If you cannot access the app, write to [CONTACTO_A_COMPLETAR] from your
account email with the subject "Delete my GainsLab account". We will process
the request and confirm through the same channel.
